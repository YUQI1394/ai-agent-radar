Privacy Policy

AI Agent Radar's public research pages can be viewed without an account. A free account is required for Saved Agents and Validation Workspace features. Authentication is provided by Supabase and, when selected, Google or GitHub. These providers process account identifiers such as your email address, provider user ID and basic profile information needed to authenticate you.

Saved Agents and Validation Workspace data—including notes, next actions, dates and decisions—are stored in Supabase and protected by row-level access rules tied to your account. A local browser copy supports temporary offline use. Exported and imported workspace backup files are created and read locally by your browser. You can remove validation records from the workspace or request account-data deletion by contacting us.

Our hosting provider may process routine technical request data, such as IP address, user agent, requested URL and timestamps, to deliver and secure the website. Public project and Issue metadata displayed by the Radar is retrieved from GitHub.

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand anonymous page visits, traffic sources and device categories. It does not use cookies and does not receive account email addresses, saved agents, search text, validation notes, interview counts, decisions or exported files.

We do not currently charge for accounts and do not request payment information. We do not sell personal information. External sites and authentication providers have their own privacy practices.
