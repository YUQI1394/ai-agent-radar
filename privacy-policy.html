Privacy Policy

AI Agent Radar's public research and validation tools can be used without an account. Favorites, validation notes, next actions and decisions created in the public tool are stored only in your browser unless you export them. They are not sent to AI Agent Radar. Clearing browser storage can remove this local data.

Legacy account pages may remain available while the service is simplified. If you previously created an account, authentication and account-linked workspace records are provided by Supabase and protected by row-level access rules. You can request deletion of legacy account data by contacting us.

Our hosting provider may process routine technical request data, such as IP address, user agent, requested URL and timestamps, to deliver and secure the website. Public project and Issue metadata displayed by the Radar is retrieved from GitHub.

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand anonymous page visits, traffic sources and device categories. It does not use cookies and does not receive account email addresses, saved agents, search text, validation notes, interview counts, decisions or exported files.

We do not sell personal information. Requests for a custom research brief begin by email; no payment details are collected by this website. External sites and service providers have their own privacy practices.
