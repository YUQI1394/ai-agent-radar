ABOUT THE RADAR

Finding useful AI agents before the noise

AI Agent Radar is an independent discovery site for newly launched autonomous tools, copilots, assistants and AI-powered workflows. The feed refreshes every six hours so builders and early adopters can discover emerging products while they are still gaining attention.

How listings are selected

We monitor relevant Product Hunt categories and identify launches connected to agents, autonomous workflows, copilots, assistants and automation. Listings are organized into practical categories including Marketing, Coding, Design and Productivity.

How Radar Score works

Radar Score combines three independent signals: community interest, launch freshness and relevance to AI-agent workflows. It is designed as a discovery aid—not a product review, paid placement or guarantee of quality.

Our principles

  • Every listing and discovery feature is free.
  • Paid placements do not influence Radar Score.
  • We do not sell user data.
  • External product information may change, so visitors should verify important details with the provider.

AI Agent Radar is an independent project and is not affiliated with Product Hunt or the listed products.
